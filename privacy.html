Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 18, 2026

Overview

Revelo: Before & After Slider ("the App") is a Shopify theme app extension developed by Ahmet Unveren. This privacy policy explains how the App handles data.

Data Collection

Revelo does not collect, store, transmit, or process any personal data from merchants or their customers. The App operates entirely as a frontend theme extension with no backend server.

How the App Works

Revelo is a pure client-side application. It renders a before and after image comparison slider using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript within a merchant's Shopify theme. No data is sent to any external server.

Cookies and Tracking

The App does not use cookies, local storage, or any form of tracking technology.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data processing services.

Merchant Data

Merchants configure the App through the Shopify theme editor. Configuration data (such as image selections and display settings) is stored by Shopify as part of the theme configuration and is subject to Shopify's privacy policy.
