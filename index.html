Revelo
Shopify Theme App Extension

Adds a drag-to-reveal before & after image comparison slider to any section of your store. No backend, no servers — works with every OS 2.0 theme.

How to set up
  1. Go to Online Store → Themes → Edit theme
  2. Navigate to any page in the editor
  3. Click Add block → Apps → Revelo: Before & After
  4. Upload your Before and After images
  5. Adjust labels, handle color and starting position
  6. Save — the slider is live instantly
